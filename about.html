About this project

A student registration interface built for an HCI laboratory activity, then inspected with Nielsen’s 10 usability heuristics and improved.

Design goals

  • Clear labels and plain wording
  • Helpful messages beside each field
  • Visible keyboard focus and readable colors
  • A layout that works on phones and computers
  • Light and dark themes
